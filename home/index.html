Break the spinning ring, bounce your shots and blast aliens and giant bosses!

  • Drag your ship and tap to fire: shots fly through the gaps of the spinning ring of blocks, bounce and hit the creatures inside.
  • A boss every 10 rounds — clear it and the two creatures it releases.
  • 5 special powers: Bomb, Electric, Pierce, Homing and the rare Big Bang; plus OVERDRIVE and ORBIT LOCK.
  • Earn coins by playing and from daily and weekly quests; unlock 12 ships, balls, trails and explosion effects.
  • Plays offline with automatic saving. Available in English, German, French, Turkish, Spanish and Portuguese (Brazil).

Free to play. Contains ads (optional rewarded ads and an interstitial after the boss every 10 rounds) and optional in-app purchases. Designed for players aged 13 and over.
